# Milky Way Unrolled

The whole Milky Way laid flat in galactic coordinates, with your horizon moving across it as a wave line through the
night. The part of the sky that never rises from your location is hatched. Pick a night, a time and a place, or press
Play to watch the horizon sweep across the band. The sky can be flattened three ways: Flat (plate carrée), or the
equal-area ovals of Mollweide and Hammer–Aitoff.

Live at https://cosmic-obs.github.io/milky-way/

The picture of the Milky Way is from NASA's Deep Star Maps 2020 (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851): NASA/Goddard Space
Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio; Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC. It is in `sky/`, reprojected for each of the three maps, at three levels of detail.

The page (`index.html`) and the picture (`sky/`) are published from the milky-way-unrolled project.
