# Milky Way Unrolled

The whole Milky Way laid flat in galactic coordinates, with your horizon moving across it as a wave line through the
night. The part of the sky that never rises from your location is hatched. Pick a night, a time and a place, or press
Play to watch the horizon sweep across the band.

Live at https://cosmic-obs.github.io/milky-way/

The page (`index.html`) is published from the milky-way-unrolled project.
